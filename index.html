Panduan Singkat Deploy Backend

1. Buka spreadsheet baru di Google Drive Anda.

2. Menu Extensions > Apps Script, tempel kode dari Code.gs.

3. Klik Deploy > New deployment > Web app.

4. Atur Execute as: Me dan Who has access: Anyone.

5. Salin tautan Web app URL (akhiran /exec) lalu tempel di atas.
